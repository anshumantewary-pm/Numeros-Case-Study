# Numeros Motors – Marketing & Brand Strategy Case Study

A marketing and brand strategy case study on **Numeros Motors**, an electric two-wheeler maker moving from a B2B fleet focus to the B2C consumer market. The company's goal, **"Mission 250"**, is to put 250,000 EVs on the road across 250+ cities. The question is how to grow that fast without diluting its core values of reliability, trust and purpose. This repository holds the team's final 7-page strategy deck, submitted as **Team Trinity (LPU)**.

## The Problem

- Shift from a B2B (fleet) customer base to aspirational B2C buyers
- Scale volume quickly on a tight marketing budget
- Get past the high upfront cost of EVs for price-sensitive segments
- Keep the brand's identity of durability and trust while scaling

## Recommendation: a Hybrid Growth Strategy

The deck recommends a **hybrid approach**: affordability and mass-market expansion for volume, combined with deeper brand trust and differentiation. It positions the two products differently:

| Product | Role | Positioning | Target segments |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Diplos Max+** | Volume and utility | Durability and trust, "Gets It Done" | MSME owners, delivery drivers, farmers, lower-middle-class buyers |
| **nFirst** | Differentiation and aspirational growth | Stability, safety and style, "Change Your Vibe" | Women riders and students |

## What the Deck Covers

1. **Market positioning and target audience.** A channel-by-product plan:
   - offline test rides: a "Load Test" challenge and "Confidence Drive" pop-ups
   - digital engagement: a "Know Your ROI" calculator and a #ChangeYourVibe UGC campaign
   - a direct-sales model through the website and app
2. **Brand identity and communication.** "Lead with engineering, end with emotion", plus a "Proven" narrative built on B2B heritage. Campaign ideas include *The Million-Mile Testimonials* and *My Ride, My Vibe*, each with a suggested tagline.
3. **Cost-effective go-to-market.** Performance marketing focused on test rides and finance enquiries, using B2B trust to win B2C buyers, and standard low-cost marketing kits for dealers.
4. **Product differentiation.** Durability as the true premium, and a "Numeros Tested for Life" badge.
5. **Long-term niche opportunities:**
   - a women's safety and mobility ecosystem (one-touch safety alert)
   - a modular cargo swap programme for delivery fleets
   - a "Mission Green Miles" sustainability initiative
6. **Financing as the critical success factor.** Battery-as-a-Service (BaaS) and subscription models, NBFC partnerships, low or zero down-payment EMIs for women and students, harvest-cycle EMIs for farmers, and a guaranteed buyback price after 3–5 years.

## Key Conclusion

Given the tight budget and high upfront EV cost, the deck names **accessible financing models** as the most important factor for success, in particular separating out the battery cost (BaaS) and offering flexible EMIs. These would make ownership affordable across all target segments and support Mission 250 without diluting the brand.
